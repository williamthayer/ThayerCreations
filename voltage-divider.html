Voltage Divider

Enter any three of the source voltage, resistor values, and output voltage (or percentage drop) to solve the divider. Resistors default to kΩ.

Divider calculator

Enter the known values and calculate. Output voltage and percentage drop describe the same target, so filling one dims the other. Resistor tolerance is applied independently to R1 and R2 for worst-case output bounds.

Copyable results

For a standard divider, Vout = Vin × R2 / (R1 + R2). Choose E3, E6, or E12 when rounding to common resistor values; E6 is selected by default.
