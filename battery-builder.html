Battery Builder

Explore series voltage and size parallel cells for a battery pack.

Voltage calculator

Choose a chemistry and series configuration, then add cell specifications to estimate the pack capacity and current.

Before calculating, enter a series cell count or voltage target; clear it to switch fields. If neither is entered, four series cells are assumed. After calculating, edit either field to update the results. A minimum target rounds series cells up; a maximum limit rounds down.

Custom cell

Enter per-cell voltages at 0% and 100% for the assumed minimum and maximum; verify these are appropriate operating limits for your cell. Other rows are optional. Paste a voltage column from a spreadsheet into a voltage field. Blank rows are interpolated in the results.

Capacity calculator

Enter the capacity and continuous discharge rating of one cell. Enter a parallel cell count, minimum pack capacity, or minimum pack current to size the pack.

Before calculating, enter either a C rating or cell discharge current, and one of the parallel cell count, pack capacity, or pack current. If none is entered for parallel sizing, one cell is assumed. After calculating, edit any field to update the pack: a pack capacity or current is treated as a minimum target and rounds the parallel count up; editing a cell specification keeps the parallel count. Target fields show the achieved value when you leave them. Unknown specifications remain blank.

Voltage vs. state of charge

Horizontal: state of charge (%). Vertical: pack voltage (V). Points are illustrative estimates, not measured cell data.

Planning estimates only. Preset profiles are illustrative, not manufacturer discharge curves. Custom profiles use the values you enter. Cell voltage varies with model, load, temperature, aging and rest time; voltage alone is especially unreliable for LFP and NiMH state of charge. The endpoints are assumed operating limits, not necessarily rested voltages at 0% or 100%. Alkaline and silver oxide profiles represent primary cells: do not recharge them. Lithium-sulfur cells may also be primary; check the specific cell before charging. Check the exact cell datasheet, charger and BMS limits, wiring, fusing, thermal design, and continuous current ratings before building or charging a pack. Lead-acid cells may charge above the assumed maximum.
